ABOUT

AHMAD AIR STRIKE is a fictional arcade shooter. Pilot imaginary starfighters, fire energy bolts and defeat alien raiders and bosses.

Move with the joystick (or arrows/WASD), hold FIRE (Space), and use SPECIAL (Shift) when charged. Collect P power-ups, H repair, S shield and coins. Unlock new aircraft in the hangar.

Works fully offline. Progress is saved on this device.
